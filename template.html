Pandora, Capital Markets Day

The presentation a listed company puts in front of its investors

Capital Markets Day is where a public company sets out its strategy to analysts, press and shareholders. Every slide is read by people paid to be sceptical. We have built Pandora's across multiple years: the narrative, the slide design, the data made legible at the back of the room, and the motion that turns a claim into something people remember.

It is the same discipline as a board deck or a results presentation, with the stakes turned up and a date that cannot move. One of those years covered the launch of their lab-grown diamond line, prepared while it was still confidential.

Pandora

From a photo library to a content supply chain

Legacy production could not keep pace with a global rebrand. We built a single source of truth: a digital twin of the product range, accurate enough that one modelled piece produces every image, film and configurator view the business needs, in every market, on demand.

Once a product exists as a digital twin it stops being only a marketing asset. The same model is used to review a design before it is made: proportions, finishes and colourways assessed on screen, and problems found while they are still cheap.

BRIO

Launching a new world without losing the old one

A Swedish house founded in 1884, pivoting from the wooden railway it is known for to a new modular line at a premium price. The product mechanics needed explaining and the heritage needed protecting, at the same time.

We built a standalone character world that teaches the play pattern by telling stories in it. Real-time rendering carried the production, so the launch moved at campaign speed instead of animation speed.

What we do

Five ways to get a message across.

Decks

Presentation and deck design

Board decks, investor material, sales enablement and keynote support. Structure first, then the craft that makes an argument easy to follow at speed. Built in your template, or a better one.

Film

Conference and brand film

Opening films, strategy pieces and launch campaigns for the rooms where the year gets set. Made in house end to end, and built so regional teams can localise them without starting again.

Product

Product visualization at volume

Photoreal imagery that replaces the photoshoot. Model the product once, then generate every angle, colourway and format your channels need, in every market, without booking a studio again.

Validation

Design validation

See it before you commit to making it. A digital twin accurate enough for marketing is accurate enough to judge a design by, so proportions, materials and colourways get tested on screen while changing them still costs nothing.

Testing

Versioning and optimisation

Most agencies deliver one execution and call it done. We build assets so variants are cheap, then run enough of them to find out which message actually moves the number you care about, rather than the one that won the internal argument.

The studio

A production company, not a freelancer with a template.

Watch the reel at wangle.studio

Wangle is a Scandinavian studio in Copenhagen and Stockholm. We work with global brands that need communication assets made properly and repeatedly, not once: the annual conference, the launch, the product library every market pulls from.

What makes that work is infrastructure rather than heroics. Assets are built modular, so localising for a new market or generating a new variant is a small job instead of a new project. That is why we can hold quality across hundreds of deliverables and still hit the date.
